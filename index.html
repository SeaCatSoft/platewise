Step 1

About you

These numbers drive your energy and protein targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the formula with the best validated accuracy in current nutrition research.

Step 2

Which looks most like you today?

Pick the closest match. The body-fat estimate fine-tunes your protein target and calorie adjustment — an honest guess works well.

Step 3

What are we building toward?

Each goal changes your calories, macro split and food emphasis based on current sports-nutrition and diabetes-care guidance.

Step 4

Foods you enjoy

Your plan is built only from foods you tick. Use Standard selection for a balanced Bajan-friendly basket, then add or remove anything. Your eating style (vegan, keto, …) is chosen at the next step and filters these automatically.

Step 5

Shape your plan

Choose the length, meals per day, and an eating style — for the whole plan, or a different style each week (e.g. one keto week, then Mediterranean).

Shopping

Your shopping list

Prices come from the Government of Barbados retail price survey (askdealia.gov.bb) across 14 supermarkets — the app uses each store's latest surveyed price and picks the cheapest brand per item. Confirm the list to turn it into a checklist you can tick off in the aisle.

Note: Prices are from the official surveys of 5 Nov, 25 Nov and 12 Dec 2025 and may drift from today's shelf prices. Fresh-produce prices sold per piece were converted to per-kg using typical fruit weights; flying fish (5's) and sausage (10's) pack weights are estimates.
My progress

Track your change

Log your weight over time — losing fat or gaining muscle, the trend line tells the story. Weigh in at the same time of day (mornings work best) once or twice a week.
